  1. 1. Information We Collect

    When you enter a giveaway, subscribe to our deals newsletter, or participate in bonus entry tasks on StackDeals, we collect the following categories of information:

    • Contact Information: Your email address, verified via double opt-in confirmation.
    • Referral & Activity Data: Your unique referral code, daily check-in timestamps, completed bonus actions, video watch credits, and cumulative reward point totals.
    • Technical & Security Data: Hashed IP addresses (never stored in plain text), browser user-agent strings, country/region metadata from Cloudflare's edge network, and Cloudflare Turnstile security tokens. This data is used strictly to prevent automated bots, duplicate accounts, and referral fraud.

    We do not collect payment information, government-issued identifiers, or sensitive personal data.

  2. 2. How We Use Your Information

    • To verify your giveaway entry and manage your account.
    • To track your entry points and maintain the points ledger.
    • To conduct weighted random winner drawings and deliver digital gift card prizes.
    • To send transactional emails: verification links, rank updates, and contest countdown reminders.
    • To send curated deal newsletters (only if you opted in at signup).
    • To detect and block fraudulent, automated, or VPN/proxy-masked entries.
    • To improve the security and integrity of our sweepstakes.

    We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

  3. 3. Cookies & Local Storage

    StackDeals does not set third-party tracking cookies. We use the following client-side storage mechanisms:

    • localStorage: We store your login session token and referral attribution code in your browser's localStorage. This allows you to remain logged in across page visits and ensures referral links are properly attributed. You can clear this data at any time via your browser settings.
    • Cloudflare Turnstile: Our bot-protection service may set a session cookie necessary for the security check to function. This is not used for advertising or tracking.

    Our partner content site (WatchCinematix) may use session storage for ad frequency capping. No cross-site tracking identifiers are shared between StackDeals and its advertising partners.

  4. 4. Third-Party Service Providers

    We share limited operational data only with trusted infrastructure providers required to run the service. All providers are bound by data processing agreements:

    • Cloudflare: Edge hosting, D1 database storage, KV caching, and Turnstile bot protection. Cloudflare processes IP addresses and user-agent strings as part of standard edge routing and security.
    • Resend: Delivery of transactional verification codes and contest alert emails. Resend receives your email address and the content of the transactional message.
    • Kit (ConvertKit): Management of verified newsletter subscribers and deal broadcasts. Kit receives your email address only if you opted in to marketing communications.
    • CPAlead / Partner Networks: Pseudonymous user identifiers (subid) passed when you voluntarily choose to complete third-party promotional offers, so points can be credited to your account. No email address or personal identity is shared with offer networks.

  5. 5. Data Retention

    • Active entrant records: Retained for the duration of the campaign plus 24 months for winner verification, tax compliance, and dispute resolution.
    • Unverified / pending signups: Automatically purged after 72 hours if the verification link expires without confirmation.
    • Winner records: Retained for 36 months for legal and regulatory compliance.
    • Newsletter subscribers: Retained until you unsubscribe.
    • Security logs (hashed IPs, Turnstile tokens): Retained for 90 days, then deleted.

  6. 6. International Data Transfers

    StackDeals is operated from the United States. If you are accessing from the United Kingdom, Canada, or the European Economic Area, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

    We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and applicable adequacy decisions to ensure that international transfers of personal data are conducted in compliance with the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and Canada's PIPEDA. By using StackDeals, you consent to such transfers.

  7. 7. Your Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

    If you are a resident of the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR:

    • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
    • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal retention obligations.
    • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
    • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
    • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing.
    • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw marketing consent at any time without affecting prior processing.

    To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@getstackdeals.xyz. We will respond within 30 days.

  8. 8. Your Rights (CCPA / US Residents)

    If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

    • Right to Know: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
    • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
    • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: StackDeals does not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.
    • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

    To submit a request, email privacy@getstackdeals.xyz. We will respond within 45 days.

  9. 9. Children's Privacy

    StackDeals is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, contact us immediately at privacy@getstackdeals.xyz and we will delete the information promptly.

    • Account deletion: To request complete deletion of your giveaway account and all associated data, email privacy@getstackdeals.xyz from the email address associated with your account. We will process deletion requests within 30 days, subject to legal retention obligations for winner records.
