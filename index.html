Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free to enter?
Yes, 100% free. You get an entry just for signing up and verifying your email. Bonus tasks and referrals are completely optional ways to increase your odds.
How does the "weighted draw" work?
Each entry you earn acts like a ticket in a hat. If you have 100 entries and someone else has 10, your name is in the hat 10 times more often. However, we enforce a maximum entry cap per person to prevent grinders from monopolizing the draw.
What are the "Bonus Tasks"?
They are short, legitimate actions (like installing a free app or signing up for a newsletter) provided by our advertising partners. They take 60–90 seconds and credit entries to your account automatically upon completion.
When and how are winners notified?
Winners are drawn immediately after the countdown ends. We will contact the email address associated with the winning account within 48 hours to arrange prize delivery.
